## Research goal

We had no clear picture of our users: their motivations and barriers, the jobs they hire us for, and the stages they go through from being handed a training task to evaluating its results. The product also needed a backlog. On top of that, we were sitting on a set of hypotheses that needed testing.

## Research

We have one B2B product — the HR portal — and it holds two offerings: professions (long-form programmes) and subscription mini-courses.

The HR portal (MVP)

![The HR portal (MVP)](./01.png)

The product manager and I picked 12 users of each product, and I wrote a separate script for each group, marking the different points of contact.

![The interview script for the study](./02.png)

After the first two interviews I realised the script needed reworking: I could not get clear answers about the stages respondents go through. I made it more open-ended and stated the goal of mapping those stages up front. In that shape the interviews went much better.

## Outcome

I ended up with 6 customer journey maps. Some hypotheses were confirmed, but most were disproved. I identified the barriers users run into, singled out the most critical ones, and turned them into backlog items.
