## Task

B2B customers of Delovye Linii, one of Russia's largest logistics companies, ordered accounting documents through their personal manager. That left managers overloaded with routine work, and mistakes crept in.

## Solution

We decided to build document ordering into the customer account interface. I started by establishing which parameters users rely on when ordering documents, and what cases can come up.
